## Professional Direction

The objective of this learning domain is not limited to prompt engineering.

The broader goal is to develop the ability to:

```text
Design AI systems
→ identify their assets and trust boundaries
→ model adversarial threats
→ secure data, models, retrieval, tools, and identities
→ monitor their behaviour
→ govern their use
→ apply them safely to cybersecurity operations
```

This supports a professional positioning at the intersection of:

- Cyber Threat Intelligence;
- security architecture;
- AI security;
- vulnerability intelligence;
- SOC and incident response;
- secure cloud and application engineering.
