<summary><b>🤖 AI Security (Learning Path)</b></summary>

*Comprehensive curriculum covering AI/ML threats, Prompt Engineering, and Supply Chain security.*


| Module | Rooms | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **AI Fundamentals** | 6 | Learn the core concepts of AI and ML, and how they apply to the cybersecurity landscape. |
| **Secure AI Systems** | 5 | Understand how AI systems are architected and how to assess their security posture. |
| **Prompt Security** | 5 | Learn how attackers manipulate AI models through malicious inputs, and how to stop them. |
| **AI Supply Chain** | 5 | Explore how AI model artefacts and supply chains can be compromised and how to defend them. |
| **Data Poisoning** | 5 | Discover how attackers corrupt the data AI systems rely on, from RAG pipelines to training sets. |
